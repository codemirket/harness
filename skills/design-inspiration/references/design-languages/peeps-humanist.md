# Open Peeps — modular people must preserve the scene

Study language: **Modular humanist drawing**. This label is a curator interpretation, not an upstream standard name.

## Brief → concept → grammar

**Brief.** A fictional community welcome promises to help a visitor find a route. The drawing must communicate people attending to the same task, not merely two friendly faces beside reassuring copy.

**Concept.** A visitor holds one map edge while a guide points to a destination. A table supports the object. Inward-facing profiles, physical contact and the shared map establish the exchange before surface detail.

**Grammar.** Open Peeps presents interchangeable hair, expression, body and pose components, with Busts, Standing and Sitting ready-made sets. Modularity creates many characters; it does not by itself invent a relevant scene. This example uses original SVG bodies and head alternatives, rather than embedding or tracing library components. The authored pose and contact relationship are separate from the replaceable head choice.

## Located decisions in the original study

- `main.maproom .scene` contains one original editable SVG with separate torso, arm, forearm, hand, head, map and table paths. The map rests on a visible tabletop; overlays put each hand above its contact point.
- The visitor’s right hand supports the map near its left edge. The guide’s bent arm ends at a fingertip on the marked route. Those endpoints are drawn for this scene rather than stretching an unrelated stock pose.
- `.engaged-head` turns both profiles toward the shared task. `.failed-head` swaps only the head choices; arms, bodies, smiles, copy, finish and map remain fixed.
- `.caption` and the SVG description provide a text explanation. The illustration is supporting communication, with no invented attendance claim, photograph or endorsement.

## Attractive wrong variant and discriminating failure

Open `?variant=misleading`. The heads now face outward while the original hands still touch the same map. The drawing remains friendly, balanced and legible. It has lost mutual attention, which the headline’s help promise requires.

Cover the headline. Follow each profile’s facing direction, then the finger and map edge. Accept the correct scene only when those parts establish the same shared task. A test for smiles, two people, or touching hands alone must reject its own insufficiency. The scene is not an anatomical demonstration or measured evidence that an audience understood the metaphor.

## Transfer to a different brief

Draw two people testing a repaired bicycle together. Establish the bike’s support, one person’s grip and the other’s inspection point before choosing heads or clothing. If the available component pose cannot reach the actual part plausibly, draw an original pose or change the composition. Do not paste these map-room arms into the new scene.

**Suitable:** personas, welcome material, storyboards and community illustration. **Poor fit:** precise safety instruction, unfamiliar anatomy or gestures, or a scene whose required action is absent from the available components.
