# Fluent Emoji — material changes, semantic form persists

Study language: **Dimensional pictorial symbols**. This label is a curator interpretation, not an upstream standard name.

## Brief → concept → grammar

**Brief.** A fictional repair circle thanks a volunteer after a repair has been confirmed. Create a supporting symbol with dimensional, flat and one-colour renditions; each must still depict repair.

**Concept.** Two material halves are held together by three cross-stitches. Their joined seam is the idea. Soft light, edge depth and colour make that idea feel tactile without changing it.

**Grammar.** The pinned Fluent Emoji Party popper directory has 3D PNG, Color SVG, Flat SVG and High Contrast SVG renditions. Microsoft’s 2024 Windows article shows layered linear/radial gradients giving drawn paths the appearance of physical mass. The transfer is semantic continuity across material treatments. This original mended tile is not a copied emoji, Unicode proposal, Microsoft mark, COLRv1 font or 3D mesh.

## Located decisions in the original study

- `main.mend .hero-tile` uses original left/right tile paths, an irregular seam and three stitches. Once-per-instance gradient IDs `hero-*` and `small-*` prevent collisions across the two inline dimensional instances.
- `.renditions` keeps the joined outer form and stitch positions in the flat and one-colour versions. In one colour, background-coloured gaps preserve the seam and stitches; colour difference alone does not express the repair.
- `.mini` supplies a 32 px instance for a real use-size check. Fine highlight strokes are expendable; the three joins are not.
- `.message` provides the actual “Repair confirmed” sentence and names the illustration as a supporting expression. Nothing in an attractive symbol verifies a live event.

## Attractive wrong variant and discriminating failure

Open `?variant=misleading`. The dimensional version still shows the repair. The flat and one-colour renditions become smooth, polished tiles with the seam and stitches removed. They preserve surface kinship while losing the subject’s defining relationship.

At 108 px, locate the join and three cross-stitches in every rendition. At 32 px inspect the same critical detail. Reject a family whose flat forms no longer depict repair, even if they match its palette and rounded outline. Also reject any consumer that deletes the visible status sentence or presents the emblem after an unconfirmed repair. File names such as “high contrast” do not establish accessible meaning.

## Transfer to a different brief

Create an original symbol for a community garden’s “seed saved for next season” acknowledgement. Choose the semantic relationship first, draw it in flat form, then add material and light while preserving the relationship in one colour and at a real small size. Keep a status sentence. Do not reuse the tile, copy a stock seed emoji or assume dimension creates a truthful achievement.

**Suitable:** labelled reactions and occasional, confirmed appreciation. **Poor fit:** sole status labels, technical mechanisms, repeated celebration during recovery, or a symbol that invents an outcome.
