# Porsche: restrained industrial presentation

**Language:** Restrained industrial product presentation. This is a curator interpretation of a coherent reference instance, not a claim that the upstream uses this exact category name. Study this as an alternative direction; do not blend whole systems indiscriminately.

**Use when:** A durable object or premium service needs a concentrated explanation and one clear next decision.

**Poor fit:** A control-dense administrative task or a character-led community experience needs many equally available actions.

## Brief → concept

A visitor considering the invented Sable Arc 01 task light needs to determine whether its base and arm can fit beside a keyboard. Finish is a secondary decision. The page offers no real product or engineering guarantee.

**Concept:** One object, one useful first decision. Use spatial restraint and concentrated original product media, then expose the physical envelope before surface preference. Premium character comes from deliberate relationships, not a luxury logo.

## Source evidence → transferable grammar

**Observed source and coverage:** The official v4 landing template describes a singular campaign goal. Fresh 1280×900 viewing showed spare navigation, a large dark media stage, one primary control and rounded media framing further down. Prior typography reading distinguishes brand type and language fallbacks. These observations inform concentration and hierarchy; they do not require a car, black palette or Porsche geometry.

**Interpretation to transfer:**

- Keep navigation to a small number of meaningful routes.
- Give the physical object enough visual space to reveal its character.
- Use restrained type scale and control emphasis around one action.
- Follow atmosphere with the concrete facts needed for that action.
- Treat finish and material as secondary when physical fit governs choice.

These relationships inform the original specimen below. They do not establish task success, accessibility conformance or a right to copy the reference’s identity.

## Located decisions in the original specimen

| Location in `index.html` | Decision | Why it serves the brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.product-hero`, `.lamp` | An original full-object SVG sits beside a short proposition; its caption distinguishes illustration from scale drawing. | Media has a clear role without implying a measured engineering representation. |
| `.product-button[href="#fit"]` | The sole default primary action is “Check desk fit”. | The next action serves the actual physical decision. |
| `#fit .specs` | Reach, base diameter and maximum height use aligned numerals with adjacent mm units. | The visitor can compare a working envelope rather than choosing by finish alone. |
| `#finishes`, `#service` | Two named finishes and a native service disclosure follow dimensions. | Material choice remains available without competing with the first constraint. |

The identity, all copy, CSS, JavaScript and inline artwork are original teaching material in this repository. The content is visibly fictional. The page loads no network assets, fonts, libraries or data. Native disclosures preserve their meaning with JavaScript disabled; enhanced controls are hidden without JavaScript and the complete default facts remain readable.

## Attractive wrong variant

Open the same HTML with `?variant=misleading`. The misleading query gives the first screen to a larger atmospheric crop and directs the primary action to finishes. Fit information moves after finish and service. This remains visually refined and its facts stay true. It fails because the complete arm silhouette and clearance data no longer lead the visitor’s decision. A crop that suits an image campaign does not automatically suit physical fit.

Judge the variant against the brief at the same width and with the same facts. Do not score it by polish alone. The explanatory teaching aside is outside `main`; the actual specimen lives in `main` in both variants.

## Failure probe

Follow Check desk fit by keyboard, read all three units, then inspect the same page at 390px. The object must not create document overflow; dimensions must retain their labels and units. Distinguish the illustrative SVG from a scale drawing and the sample numbers from verified product specifications.

At narrow width also inspect the visible focus ring, long labels, enlarged text and the meaningful order of content. No critical interaction is hover-only; no animation is needed to communicate state.

## Materially different transfer task

Present a portable air-quality sensor for a facilities team. Their governing decision is placement, power and service access, not desk-lamp finish. Compose new original media and a single placement check, then show the constraints that control it. Do not copy Arc 01 geometry or treat the fictional dimensions as reusable product facts.

**Reject the transfer when:** Reject a transfer where a dramatic crop hides the connector or mounting clearance, or where atmosphere precedes a constraint needed to choose the device.
