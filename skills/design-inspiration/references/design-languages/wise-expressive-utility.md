# Wise: expressive numerical utility

**Language:** Bold consumer utility. This is a curator interpretation of a coherent reference instance, not a claim that the upstream uses this exact category name. Study this as an alternative direction; do not blend whole systems indiscriminately.

**Use when:** A practical consumer calculator or pricing decision needs both a memorable public voice and explicit economics.

**Poor fit:** A quiet reading environment or a many-column operator comparison needs lower expressive emphasis.

## Brief → concept

A visitor to the invented Loop Club needs to choose whole workshop hours and understand the complete monthly example cost, including a fixed fee and expiring allowance. The sample must not look like a real membership offer.

**Concept:** A bold invitation attached to transparent utility. Let expressive scale attract attention, but put the full recurring amount and its arithmetic beside review. The governing relationship is amount plus disclosure, not Wise’s green.

## Source evidence → transferable grammar

**Observed source and coverage:** Prior official Brand/product inspection observed large display type, a confident accent, rounded controls and paired amounts with fees/arrival information before the money action. Fresh 1280×900 viewing confirmed the bold public type and contrast of the current UK homepage; the current detail attempt remained on the hero after cookie dismissal, so it does not add a fresh calculator state. Public Docs was lock-marked in prior evidence; no current component-library coverage is claimed.

**Interpretation to transfer:**

- Use a distinct display voice without making every value compete.
- Make the object of the decision numerically dominant.
- Place mandatory fees and material terms next to review.
- Reconcile arithmetic at the same grain and time period.
- Use clear native input and explicit invalid-state feedback.

These relationships inform the original specimen below. They do not establish task success, accessibility conformance or a right to copy the reference’s identity.

## Located decisions in the original specimen

| Location in `index.html` | Decision | Why it serves the brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.utility-copy h1`, `.calculator` | A heavy public headline sits beside a bounded, rounded utility panel with an original yellow/indigo identity. | Expression and task have related but distinct roles; no Wise asset is needed. |
| `#hours`, `#hours-help` | Whole hours 1–40 use a labelled native number control; invalid values retain the last valid total with an explicit warning. | Input constraints and displayed calculation state do not silently diverge. |
| `.cost-lines`, `#total` | The default 8 × £6 + £4 = £52 monthly amount is shown as parts and total. | The price’s recurring grain and fixed fee are visible before review. |
| `.plan-note`, `#receipt` | Expiry and no-rollover terms appear before the action and are repeated in local review. | Review is a real page-state change without implying a booking or payment. |

The identity, all copy, CSS, JavaScript and inline artwork are original teaching material in this repository. The content is visibly fictional. The page loads no network assets, fonts, libraries or data. Native disclosures preserve their meaning with JavaScript disabled; enhanced controls are hidden without JavaScript and the complete default facts remain readable.

## Attractive wrong variant

Open the same HTML with `?variant=misleading`. The misleading query makes the true £6 hourly rate enormous, reduces the full monthly total and moves the price breakdown behind a disclosure after the review button. All arithmetic and facts remain available. It fails because the easiest number to see is not the amount the visitor must judge; a recurring fee becomes a secondary discovery.

Judge the variant against the brief at the same width and with the same facts. Do not score it by polish alone. The explanatory teaching aside is outside `main`; the actual specimen lives in `main` in both variants.

## Failure probe

Change 8 hours to 12: hours cost must become £72 and full monthly total £76. Review must repeat 12 × £6 + £4 with expiry. Enter 0, 1.5 or 41: review must be unavailable and stale review must close. Return to 1 hour: the total is £10. At 390px, fee and total must stay adjacent to the action.

At narrow width also inspect the visible focus ring, long labels, enlarged text and the meaningful order of content. No critical interaction is hover-only; no animation is needed to communicate state.

## Materially different transfer task

Build a household-energy estimate with a standing daily charge and a usage charge over a stated number of days. Use verified or clearly fictional tariffs; show units, period and uncertainty before an example review. This differs from membership hours because the estimate depends on consumption assumptions. Do not copy Loop Club’s rate or imply a real financial saving.

**Reject the transfer when:** Reject a transfer that promotes the unit rate while hiding the standing charge, or that compares totals over different periods.
