What is included in this fictional pass?

The chosen number of workshop hours and basic access to the shared space. Materials and training are outside this sample. No real availability is implied.

How is the sample total calculated?

Multiply the selected whole hours by £6, then add the fixed £4 monthly fee. At 8 hours, £48 + £4 = £52. Nothing is saved between visits.

Design-language study: Bold consumer utility

Original fictional content and identity. This page is a local teaching specimen; it sends no data and makes no real offer. System fonts and original inline artwork only.

Purposeful variant. Expressive type and a confident accent invite use, while the full monthly total, fee and expiry rule sit beside the decision. The cost reconciles as hours × rate + fixed fee.

Attractive wrong variant. A giant true hourly rate becomes the price anchor, the total shrinks and its breakdown moves behind a disclosure after the review action. Nothing is numerically false, yet the visual hierarchy makes the recurring economics harder to judge.
