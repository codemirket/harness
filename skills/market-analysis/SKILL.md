# Market analysis

Define the decision and the market before estimating its size. Preserve the
difference between available demand, reachable buyers and achievable sales.

## Define the measurement

Identify the buyer, buying unit, use case, geography, period, channel and price
basis. State exclusions and whether the output measures customers, locations,
transactions, spend, GMV or supplier revenue. A company, user and branch are
different denominators. Specify the date of a stock measure and the period of
a flow measure.

Use current sources suited to the question: official statistics, filings,
procurement data, observed transactions, customer research and directly inspected
competitor offers. Search for contrary evidence. Keep source URL, publication
and observation dates, table or passage, geography, sample and measurement unit
beside material inputs. Distinguish vendor claims from observed performance and
several reports repeating one source from independent corroboration.

## Build and challenge the estimate

- Start with compatible buyer counts and realistic spend or unit volume. Show
  the arithmetic and ranges. Separate an observed input from a modeling choice.
- Define TAM as the economic opportunity within the stated market. Calculate SAM
  from the portion the product can serve under actual product, regulatory,
  language, geographic and distribution constraints. Segment count is not a
  valid proxy for segment value.
- Reconcile segment overlaps before adding them. When multiplying filters, check
  that each applies to the previous denominator; do not assume independence of
  correlated traits. Explain which excluded or unmeasured populations remain.
- Build SOM from an explicit time horizon and operating capacity: eligible unique
  prospects, reachable channels, conversion evidence, sales cycle, retention and
  delivery limits. Deduplicate contacts across channels. Use a generic market
  share percentage only as a labeled sensitivity, not as evidence of reach.
- Distinguish exit recurring run rate, contracted value, recognized revenue and
  cash. An annual price multiplied by customers won late in the year does not
  equal revenue earned throughout that year.
- Triangulate with another method when it can expose a material error. Explain
  mismatched definitions and uncertainty rather than averaging incompatible
  estimates or declaring confidence from a fixed source count.

For market-sizing work, read the
[worked denominator and channel case](references/market-boundaries.md).

## Compare alternatives fairly

Inspect current offers and material terms. Date price, feature and availability
claims; an absent public statement is not proof that a capability is absent.
Compare substitutes and the current workaround as well as direct competitors.
Distinguish interview statements, stated willingness to pay and actual buying
behavior. Do not make a convenient research sample representative by assertion.

## Deliver

Lead with the supported opportunity or constraint and what would change the
decision. Supply the estimate, definitions, source trail, sensitivity and the
largest unresolved assumption. Recommend the next evidence-gathering step when
its value is clear; do not fabricate missing numbers to complete a framework.
Research does not authorize paid data purchases, prospect contact or campaigns.
