# Construct a visual explanation

Use for editorial illustration, product mechanisms and artwork whose parts must
move coherently. Derive the visual idea from the subject and message; do not adopt
a fixed isometric style, character, path count or level of detail from a reference.

For each meaningful section or shot, identify the claim or relationship, the
subject/action that makes it visible, and its placement with the actual copy.
A metaphor should help explain that relationship. If the picture would communicate
the same thing for an unrelated product, reconsider the subject. A product claim
needs a real product state or evidence; label illustrative mechanisms as such.

Choose one model of the subject before drawing pieces. Record its local coordinates,
attachments, shared landmarks, depth/occlusion order and meaningful degrees of
freedom. Give each editable group a semantic role. Derive a label, gauge or readout
from the same state that drives the geometry; do not animate a number independently
and call it a simulation. Technical accuracy needs domain evidence beyond a plausible
shape. Avoid details that imply a measured or anatomically accurate model without it.

Inspect the construction at its delivery size: silhouette, joins, contact, overlap,
clearance and focal detail. If parts move, test both directions, extreme poses and
intermediate collisions. A piston must stay attached; a hand must keep its grip;
a foreground object must occlude the right part throughout the motion. Shared
coordinates and a deliberate transform hierarchy often fix these defects better
than additional easing. Keep motion's timing contract with its scene owner.

For SVG retain editable master groups, meaningful IDs and hidden states. Optimize
a delivery copy only after comparing renders and exercising behavior. Two instances
must not share conflicting mask/gradient/clip IDs. A pixel-perfect resting preview
does not prove preserved interaction, hidden states or future editability.

For article or campaign illustration, use the paragraph/shot purpose to choose crop,
scale and image/text balance. Check the narrow placement before multiplying assets.
Keep exact brand marks and text in their proper source layers. No minimum number of
parts, fixed canvas size or compulsory animation establishes quality.
