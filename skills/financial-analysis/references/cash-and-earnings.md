# Worked case: profit with a cash constraint

Use this case when checking burn signs, cash timing or an apparent disagreement
between profitable operations and short runway. All figures are fictional USD.
Assume no tax, depreciation or interest for this limited example.

The business starts July with $120,000 of unrestricted cash and needs a $25,000
minimum balance. It recognizes $80,000 of July service revenue, collects $40,000,
and recognizes and pays $60,000 of operating expense. It also pays $30,000 for
equipment and repays $10,000 of loan principal. There is no opening receivable.

| Item | July | August | September |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Opening cash | 120,000 | 60,000 | 40,000 |
| Collections | 40,000 | 50,000 | 50,000 |
| Operating cash payments | (60,000) | (60,000) | (60,000) |
| Equipment purchase | (30,000) | 0 | 0 |
| Loan principal | (10,000) | (10,000) | (10,000) |
| Closing cash | 60,000 | 40,000 | 20,000 |

July's operating result on these assumptions is +$20,000, while operating cash
flow is -$20,000 and total cash movement is -$60,000. The $40,000 receivable
explains the revenue/collection difference. Equipment and principal consume cash
without being July operating expenses in this example.

The minimum cash balance is breached in September, even though cash stays
positive. Dividing opening cash by a negative revenue-minus-expenses number is
not a meaningful runway calculation. Nor should July's one-off equipment payment
be assumed to repeat indefinitely.

In a collection downside, change July collections to $20,000 and August and
September to $30,000 each, with all other cash flows unchanged. Closing balances
become $40,000, $0 and -$40,000: the constraint moves to August. A negative balance
indicates a funding gap, not an executable bank balance. No financing is assumed.

An acceptable analysis explains these bridges and the required response before
the breach. It does not infer full-year earnings, long-term viability or a
specific financing recommendation from three months of cash data.
