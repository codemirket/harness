01 / The changing hour

Nothing holds still. Neither does the light.

Same place.
Another world.

A window. A wall. The edge of a familiar street. Come back an hour later and the ordinary has rearranged itself.

Try this: stay long enough to see it change.

Notes for the noticing.

Three ways to begin

01

Follow a shadow.

Find a shape on the ground. Look for the thing that made it. Notice how far apart they are.

A two-minute practice

Choose a still object near a window. Mark its shadow with your eye, then return a few minutes later. The movement is small. That is the point.

02

Collect a color.

Pick one color from the day. Find it again in three unexpected places. Keep the differences.

A two-minute practice

Place a plain sheet of paper beside something colorful. Compare the lit and shaded sides. Name the colors you actually see, rather than the color you expect.

03

Wait at the edge.

Find where the light ends and the shade begins. Some edges are sharp. Others disappear slowly.

A two-minute practice

Look at the same edge from near and far. Try describing its softness without taking a photograph. You do not need the perfect words to pay attention.
