02 / A SIMPLE PRACTICE

A quieter start.
A clearer finish.

There is no perfect process.
Just a few useful conditions.

  1. 01 — BEFORE

    Name the work.

    Write one sentence about what you want to move forward. Make it small enough to begin.

  2. 02 — DURING

    Protect the space.

    Silence the interruptions you can. Keep a scrap of paper for the thoughts that can wait.

  3. 03 — AFTER

    Leave a thread.

    Note the next step before you stop. Give your future self an easy place to return.
