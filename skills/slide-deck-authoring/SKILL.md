# Slide deck authoring

Establish audience, purpose, duration, key claim and the decision/action expected. Inspect source figures, dates, assets and any existing deck/template. Use the actual available Codex host presentation tools; no Mirket Office runtime is implied.

Build a coherent narrative with a clear point per slide and purposeful variation in evidence/layout. Keep text, charts and diagram elements editable when requested; a full-slide bitmap is not equivalent. Preserve brand/asset rights and do not invent metrics or claims to fill a layout.

Save, reopen and render every slide. Inspect hierarchy, alignment, font/glyph availability, clipping, contrast, chart labels and consistent source values. Verify speaker notes, links, transitions or export behavior only when requested and supported. Recheck affected slides after changes.

Return the editable deck, requested export, rendered-slide evidence and source/feature limits. Unless the task sets another budget, share 30 minutes and three final repairs across deck production and review, with one target repair; combined motion work uses 45 minutes/four, not an added budget. Stop at that limit or a concrete capability gap and report unresolved defects. A saved file or pleasing cover slide does not establish the deck's full usability or factual accuracy.
