# Bottleneck diagnosis

Keep a project-local `product/funnel-diagnosis.json` with the eligible and
converted counts, reproducible rate, traffic context, measurement status, and
one selected bottleneck. Diagnose the narrowest observed stage before changing
headline, visual polish, or acquisition spend. A strong acquisition rate and a
weak activation rate call for an activation hypothesis, not generic CRO.

State whether the bottleneck is an observation or a causal result. Instrument
health, event identity, denominator, traffic mix, and accessibility are
guardrails; they are not evidence of lift. A proposed change gets a predeclared
success metric and a guardrail, but this artifact must not claim a causal win
without a valid comparison. See the stage-specific approach in the [Reforge
case report, 2025-02-27](https://growth-designers.beehiiv.com/p/newsletter-reforge-case-study-february).

Repository qualification tooling catches arithmetic and an incorrectly
selected lowest stage. It cannot decide whether the stage is important to
users or whether the proposed repair will work.
