# Vector artwork

Inspect the target medium, view box, display sizes, stroke/fill language and existing icon/asset system. Start with a minimal rendered SVG specimen or supplied reference. Preserve editable geometry and meaningful grouping; do not substitute an embedded bitmap for requested vector source.

Author editable SVG through the available host file tools, then use qualified Mirket SVGO hygiene and resvg preview routes. Keep IDs, masks, gradients, clipping, symbol references and accessibility semantics intact during optimization. Verify external references and active content against the intended use boundary. Reference examples grant no right to copy protected artwork.

When incorporating permitted third-party geometry, fonts or components, preserve their source, license and required attribution/notices through optimization and handoff; removing metadata must not discard the only required credit.

Render the saved source before and after optimization at intended and pressure sizes. Inspect silhouette, optical alignment, stroke weight, alpha, cropping and sprite/use behavior where applicable. Compare exports in the actual consuming context; a smaller byte count does not prove equivalent pixels or meaning.

Deliver editable SVG/sprite source, optimized artifact, previews and consumer evidence. Unless the task sets another budget, share 15 minutes and two final repairs across vector production and review, with one target repair; combined interface/paged work uses 30 minutes/three and motion uses 45 minutes/four, not their sum. Stop when requested variants and fidelity checks pass or report unsupported features and remaining visual defects at the limit. Preserve originals on failed optimization.

## Illustration mode

Use this mode for an editable spot illustration, character scene, onboarding
card, empty state, or other artful vector composition. For diagrams, logos,
icons, or an already-approved interface direction, keep their narrower skill
and semantic owner. Read [illustration-craft.md](references/illustration-craft.md)
when the brief needs a style decision, a figure, or a detail-level visual
review; do not load it for a mechanical SVG optimization.

1. Translate the brief into a small style contract: subject, audience/medium,
   focal object or relationship, palette roles, line/fill language,
   figure/prop treatment when present, composition, text limits, and forbidden
   motifs. If a supplied reference or an approved style family exists, measure
   and follow its relationships; combine families only when the brief calls for
   a deliberate hybrid, and do not infer universal proportions from one
   example.
2. Make the decision before drawing: choose the family or deliberate hybrid
   whose silhouette, density, texture, and degree of outline serve the subject
   and display size. For figurative spot art, start with one legible action and,
   where useful, one hero prop. Abstract, decorative, and multi-object briefs
   may need a different block-in. Check silhouette, negative space, reading
   order, and contact before adding detail.
3. For a figurative action, construct the pose from weight-bearing geometry
   outward. When hand anatomy or a grip is material to the brief, check
   left/right, palm/back, finger direction and thumb side, with a connected
   wrist and forearm. Preserve intentional abstraction in tiny or mitten-like
   hands; do not add anatomy the style does not need. Make overlaps and contact
   with feet, seats, shadows and props explain the action rather than float.
4. Add the style-specific parts only after the block-in reads at intended size:
   hair/face, clothing folds, props, accents, and short labels. Preserve
   editable paths, meaningful groups, stable IDs, and accessible text/labels
   where text is required. Do not copy a reference’s protected artwork, asset,
   font, or distinctive mark; learn its construction relationship instead.
5. Render the saved source with the qualified Mirket resvg route, inspect the
   displayed pixels with an image-capable tool, then use the qualified Mirket
   SVGO route for hygiene/optimization and render again. Compare the intended
   size and at least one pressure size; zoom hands, faces, feet, overlaps,
   contacts, and any small text from the saved vector. A successful render,
   optimizer receipt, or XML check is not visual proof.
6. For consequential creative work, use a reviewer of comparable capability
   who sees the artifact and rubric, not the author’s rationale. Keep one
   task-level production/review/repair budget across specimens or delegates;
   do not reset it by making a new card. Repair only findings tied to the
   contract or an observed defect, then rerun affected views and disclose
   unsupported features or uninspected pixels.

Return the brief/style decision, editable SVG, optimized artifact, preview
paths, inspected sizes/regions, and remaining defects. If the qualified Mirket
render or image-capable view is unavailable, report visual QA as untested rather
than claiming illustration fidelity.
