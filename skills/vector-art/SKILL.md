# Vector artwork

Inspect the target medium, view box, display sizes, stroke/fill language and existing icon/asset system. Start with a minimal rendered SVG specimen or supplied reference. Preserve editable geometry and meaningful grouping; do not substitute an embedded bitmap for requested vector source.

Author editable SVG through the available host file tools, then use qualified Mirket SVGO hygiene and resvg preview routes. Keep IDs, masks, gradients, clipping, symbol references and accessibility semantics intact during optimization. Verify external references and active content against the intended use boundary. Reference examples grant no right to copy protected artwork.

Render the saved source before and after optimization at intended and pressure sizes. Inspect silhouette, optical alignment, stroke weight, alpha, cropping and sprite/use behavior where applicable. Compare exports in the actual consuming context; a smaller byte count does not prove equivalent pixels or meaning.

Deliver editable SVG/sprite source, optimized artifact, previews and consumer evidence. Unless the task sets another budget, share 15 minutes and two final repairs across vector production and review, with one target repair; combined interface/paged work uses 30 minutes/three and motion uses 45 minutes/four, not their sum. Stop when requested variants and fidelity checks pass or report unsupported features and remaining visual defects at the limit. Preserve originals on failed optimization.
