for this repository. Eight project bodies are classified method-adapted because
they retain independently worded techniques from reviewed source families; no
external prose, code, workflow, asset or font has been copied into these bodies.

The original text is covered by the repository [MIT license](../LICENSE).
External source selection is a discovery/comparison record, not permission to
redistribute. The 2026-10-27 retrieval and body review is retained in
`planning/reviews/2026-10-27/`; its receipts and focused source analyses record
what was actually fetched, which source file/section was inspected, and which
license evidence remains unverified.

## Rights and adaptation boundary

Most bodies are independently-authored; eight project bodies are explicitly
classified method-adapted with reviewed, independently worded mappings. The
reference index preserves all selected families and URLs for comparison or
focused method review. URLs with moving revisions are not source pins.
No framework, executable, package, account, provider or runtime is adopted by a
skill or a reference. Runtime availability must be qualified independently.

Anthropic DOCX material remains link-only because its retained license restricts
derivatives/distribution. Trail of Bits material remains link-only; the local
reference plan records CC-BY-SA-4.0, which cannot be silently treated as MIT.
No quotations or adapted instructions from either source are included.
Brands, artwork, fonts and library examples require separate exact reuse rights.
W3C criteria can be consulted by link; no conformance prose is reproduced.

Before any future adaptation, record the exact reviewed URL/revision, path and
heading, retained technique, terms/notices, classification, removed host/runtime
assumptions, destination section and observable acceptance case. If those are
unverified, keep the material comparison-only/link-only rather than claiming adoption.
