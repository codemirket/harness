# Art direction and rendered critique

Use when interpreting a reference or when the requested improvement depends on
visual judgment. The surface-specific composition reference still supplies the
layout craft. This reference makes direction and criticism specific enough to act
on; it adds no mandatory artifact, style package or extra review round.

## Translate the reference into decisions

Determine whether the user wants faithful reproduction, a related direction or
inspiration for one aspect. Inspect the actual reference and state which parts
were available. A screenshot supplies a particular composition and state; it does
not establish offscreen content, responsive behavior or interactions.

Describe a few relationships that create the effect before choosing tokens:

| What to inspect | Useful translation into the current project |
| --- | --- |
| First read and sequence | Which content leads, what supports it, where the reader goes next |
| Type and density | Contrast between roles, line lengths, pace and amount of information visible |
| Image and text | Subject, crop, scale, caption relationship and whether the asset carries evidence or atmosphere |
| Repeated details | Alignment, spacing rhythm, borders, shapes and states that make separate pieces belong together |
| Motion, when observable | The event it explains, what stays stable and how it returns to a usable state |

Carry over relationships that serve the new subject. Derive specifics from its
brand, content and task. A large art image can lead a gallery but cannot replace
the amounts an operator must compare. Several references may inform different
aspects; reconcile them into one direction rather than layering complete systems.

For faithful reproduction, retain the supplied geometry, assets, typography and
states where available. Compare at the reference's viewport and content before
making a fidelity claim. Missing font files or unknown interaction states are
specific limitations. For interpretation, explain the adapted decisions without
claiming pixel identity. Do not replace a requested close match with a new concept.

Use existing task notes for a compact direction, for example:

> The archive's maps and specimen captions lead. Keep the supplied serif headings,
> align captions to image edges and let short catalog facts sit closer together
> than essay paragraphs. Preserve the searchable index and its current filters.

This predicts visible choices. “Premium,” “modern” or “less generic” alone does
not. Familiar fonts, cards or restrained layouts can be the right choices; assess
their execution and fit. Distinctiveness can come from actual content and precise
relationships without replacing the established visual system.

## Turn a visual judgment into a correction

Inspect the artifact, not only its source or the builder's description. For a
substantial design review, keep the observation, proposed remedy and inspected
result together in existing task notes. Capture only what makes the judgment
reproducible: route or artifact, state, viewport/container, theme and content when
they affect it. A screenshot path is a locator, not an explanation.

Anchor criticism to a relationship or a task consequence:

| Weak criticism | Actionable observation and check |
| --- | --- |
| “The form needs polish.” | At 390px with a long supplier name, contact notes compete with the identity. Reduce the notes heading's emphasis and recheck the same filled and invalid states; retain the Save action. |
| “The hero feels generic.” | The brief is about a physical collection, but the opening shows only abstract shapes. Use the supplied object photograph with its caption, then inspect its crop and the actual opening text on wide and narrow screens. |
| “Make the diagram professional.” | At its embedded width, two leader lines cross the same label and obscure the relationship. Reroute those lines and inspect both the small silhouette and the readable labeled view. |

Separate a broken requirement from an aesthetic preference. “The price disappears
at the supported width” is a defect. “A quieter border would give the price more
emphasis” is a proposal whose value depends on the direction. Preserve a working
choice when the alternative has no clearer reason or inspected benefit.

When a separate critic is useful, give them the brief, direction and actual
artifact before the maker's quality verdict. Ask for locations and consequences,
not an unexplained taste score. Resolve disagreements against the task and render;
an agent's favorable opinion does not establish owner acceptance.

## Check whether the correction worked

Compare matched content and states after changing the responsible relationship.
Judge both the intended gain and its cost: a calmer heading may weaken identity;
a tighter crop may remove useful context; greater density may harm touch use.
Inspect the affected continuation, sibling component or responsive state when the
same rule reaches it. Avoid treating a good opening image as a whole-page review.

Retain the best-supported version. Repeated cosmetic edits without a new
observation are a reason to revisit the direction or missing reference, not proof
that more variants will improve it. Report which visible problem improved, which
tradeoff remains and which states were not inspected. Artifact inspection,
interaction verification and the owner's preference answer different questions.
