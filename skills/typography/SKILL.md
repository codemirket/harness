# Typography

Inspect the content's language, reading distance, density and medium. Identify the hierarchy needed for headings, body text, labels, numbers and supporting text. Preserve meaningful emphasis and source wording while shaping rhythm.

Choose from available fonts with verified use rights and glyph coverage. A reference to a design system grants no font redistribution right. Inspect fallback behavior; do not rely on an unavailable typeface or synthesize a script it cannot render. Tune size, weight, measure, line height and spacing together rather than adjusting a single scale in isolation.

Judge hierarchy with the actual text. Check whether adjacent roles remain distinct through size, weight, spacing and placement without relying only on color. Use tabular or proportional numerals according to the comparison task; check currency, signs, decimals and units together. Code, tables and compact labels need their own pressure cases. Preserve readable body measure and paragraph rhythm before ornamenting display text.

Create a specimen with real long titles, paragraphs, numerals, links and the relevant scripts. Inspect wrapping, truncation, baseline alignment, contrast, zoom and narrow layouts; for exports check the saved output rather than only the authoring canvas. Use hierarchy and whitespace before adding ornamental styles.

Verify text shaping in the consuming renderer: joined scripts, combining marks, ligatures, bidirectional runs, fallback glyphs and mixed-script baselines where relevant. Do not split a grapheme or joining sequence to animate characters. A browser specimen does not establish a design-file, SVG or PDF renderer's shaping support. When the target supports only fixed single-line text, report that limit instead of promising paragraph layout.

For moving type, inspect readable settled frames and the time available to read each message at normal speed. For a font or layout change, compare before/after wraps and text block heights so changed metrics do not conceal content or move actions unexpectedly. Confirm the saved export uses the intended available font or an explicitly accepted fallback.

The approved [Geist typography](https://vercel.com/geist/typography) and [GOV.UK typeface](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/typeface/) pages are optional, moving comparison references for code/product and public-service reading surfaces respectively. Inspect the current relevant section when consulted; these locators carry no font license or universal scale to impose.

Return the specimen/source, chosen hierarchy, font availability/rights and inspected conditions. Unless the task sets another budget, share 15 minutes/two final repairs for static specimens, 30 minutes/three for interfaces or pages, and 45 minutes/four for moving type; use the largest applicable limit and allow one target repair. Stop when the reading surface is stable or report unresolved font/glyph/rendering evidence at the limit. Do not impose an unrelated brand's typography.
