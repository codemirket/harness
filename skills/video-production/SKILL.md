# Video production

Establish duration, aspect ratio, frame rate, narrative beats, output formats and playback targets. Keep an editable project-owned HyperFrames HTML/CSS/JS composition, local vector/Lottie/media assets, supplied audio and independently editable caption sources. Inspect a short native specimen before expanding.

Use only the qualified Mirket private Chromium capture and controlled FFmpeg/ffprobe route. Verify the exact available build/format capability. No browser-free HyperFrames path, personal Chrome, second renderer or first-use download is implied. Refuse missing assets, dynamic remote dependencies and unsupported effects explicitly.

For an authored HTML composition, read [the pinned HyperFrames 0.8.197 authoring contract](references/hyperframes-0.8.197-authoring.md) before preparing a render. It gives the exact composition-root shape, local-asset rules, no-timeline readiness rule and Anime registration check. It does not grant host approval or turn a receipt into proof that the intended animation moved.

Render a bounded sample, inspect typography/composition/pacing, then export the requested deliverables. Verify saved metadata and decode actual output: frame order/count and timing, changed-source/changed-pixels behavior, transitions, final frame, seek/playback, supplied audio sync/volume and caption content/timing. Check GIF timing and ordered PNG sequences when requested. Claim transparency only for the tested codec/player combination.

Return editable sources/assets, checked exports and frame/playback/audio/caption evidence. Unless the task sets another budget, share 45 minutes and four final repairs across motion production and review, with one target repair. Stop on a material capability/rights gap or at that limit and report unresolved defects. Reconcile uncertain finalization before rerendering or overwriting output.
