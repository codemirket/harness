# Marketing copy

Establish the audience, situation, offer, voice, surface and intended action. Read existing positioning and real product evidence before drafting. Use specific benefits the audience can understand; connect feature claims to what the person can accomplish.

Write a clear hierarchy from promise to supporting detail, proof and next action. Use real product terms and truthful constraints. Verify claims, comparisons, prices and dates that could have changed. Do not fabricate testimonials, scarcity, metrics or customer outcomes to make copy persuasive.

Review the draft as a user journey: what is offered, for whom, why trust it, what happens next and which doubts remain. For an existing UI/page, check whether the actual space and interaction support the wording. Preserve explicit tone and legal/source text.

Return usable copy and any claims requiring proof or a product decision. Stop when the draft fits the brief and its material claims are checked; name missing facts rather than publishing invented certainty. Sending, publication or campaign changes require their own authorization.

Before calling copy ready, load `references/claim-proof-review.md` and review the exact saved artifact through a project-local `product/copy-review.json`. Record a digest and proof refs for each material claim. Fail closed on unsupported testimonials and expired metrics; unknown or rejected claims must be absent from saved copy. The checker verifies these boundaries only—it cannot prove persuasion or legal sufficiency.
