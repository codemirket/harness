# Current position and future point of view

Keep `product/positioning.json` split into `current` and
`future_point_of_view`. Current positioning names the audience, use situation,
real alternatives buyers consider, differentiated value, and proof. Every
current alternative needs buyer evidence; a competitor that is merely on a
roadmap or in a trend report is a future risk, not a current alternative.

The future section can state a point of view and monitor future competitors,
but it must not silently change today's message or imply buyer adoption. This
separation is especially important for AI categories, where a plausible future
capability can be mistaken for present value. Revisit the current section after
win/loss, advisory, usage, or support evidence—not after a competitor mention
alone.

Use proof references for every public claim and preserve the rejected or
unsubstantiated alternatives in the decision record. See [April Dunford,
2026-05-21](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/positioning-in-the-age-of-ai) and
[April Dunford, 2026-06-11](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/in-the-age-of-ai-you-need-a-point).
