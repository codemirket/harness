# SVG and Lottie animation

Inspect the target player, output dimensions, duration, frame rate, looping and intended use. Inventory shapes, masks, gradients, text, effects and external assets against the actual supported player/export subset. Do not infer universal Lottie or SVG support from one preview.

Keep editable vector/timing source and local assets with verified rights. Prefer simple geometry and explicit property ownership where it improves portability. Use the qualified Mirket vector/video route for operations it actually supports; do not introduce a second animation renderer or remote dependency to hide a feature gap.

Preview the saved source in the intended player and compare representative start, action and resting frames with any export. Check loop seams, direction, alpha, clipping, font substitution and reduced-motion/static fallback when interactive use requires it. Change a source property and confirm the decoded output changes accordingly.

Deliver editable source, assets, tested feature/player combinations and preview/export evidence. Unless the task sets another budget, share 45 minutes and four final repairs across motion production and review, with one target repair. Stop on unsupported required features or at the limit and preserve the original; report a precise gap and unresolved defects instead of silently flattening or substituting the animation.
