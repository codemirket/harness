# Skill provenance inputs

These are active source/license receipts and source-to-method mappings used by
`../../../scripts/refresh-skill-catalog.py`. They establish the provenance of
current independently worded skills; they are not an implementation backlog or
proof that every skill improves model outcomes. Receipt observation dates remain
part of their evidence identity.

[Reference selection](../../../docs/references.md),
[skill contract](../../../docs/skills.md), and
[provenance ledger](../../../skills/PROVENANCE.md) own the current interpretation.
Raw upstream bodies remain outside shipped source. Refreshing hashes does not
grant rights, change admission or complete an acceptance test.
