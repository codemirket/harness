The pinned evidence exposes three authoring defects in the catalog’s current
provenance language. First, the selected Matt and ECC files are not the
engineering/security bodies described by their family labels; those mappings
must be marked unverified instead of being presented as source fidelity.
Second, README inventories (OpenAI, Google, LangChain, LangGraph, Promptfoo,
Stagehand and Crawl4AI) support only the narrow sections named above; they do
not authorize adopting their installers, services, model routers, crawlers or
telemetry. Third, restricted or component-scoped licenses (Anthropic DOCX,
Serena GPL application, CUA FSL/AGPL components, Skyvern AGPL and
Trail-of-Bits CC-BY-SA) require link-only treatment unless separate rights are
recorded.

The local skill bodies are mostly independently authored and preserve the
important boundary: Codex owns browser, SQL, documents, permissions, shell,
Git and native agents; Mirket owns only its qualified memory, JS/TS,
design/video and receipts. No production skill edit is made by this reference
review because the observed defects are provenance classifications and missing
source verification, not a demonstrated behavior failure in the local bodies.

The exact body review remains time-bounded for the unpinned Agent Skills and
WCAG web pages if their HTML changes after this date. The approved whole-tree
locators for Matt and ECC were traversed and the engineering/security bodies
listed above supersede the collector's arbitrary first-file receipts. These
are explicit qualification limits, not reasons to invent a successful review.
