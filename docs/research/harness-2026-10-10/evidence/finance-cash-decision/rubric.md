# Finance decision review

Inspect the actual program, source, results and decision. Check that the brief
connects profitable operations to the observed liquidity shortfall, treats debt
principal correctly, distinguishes minimum financing for zero cash from the chosen
reserve, and explains the delayed-receipts sensitivity. Monthly endpoints cannot
prove intramonth liquidity or an exact runway beyond the horizon. The recommendation
must preserve owner authority and mark all inputs as synthetic. Deterministic
arithmetic passing does not establish financial decision quality or workbook fidelity.
