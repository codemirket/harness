Reviewed 2026-10-10. This is source inspection evidence, not a claim that every skill was audited or that any upstream runtime was tested.

Each GitHub repository was fetched at the exact commit below. Its complete archive path inventory was counted; selected skill bodies, companions, helpers, manifests, hooks and licenses were then inspected. Large collections were sampled around the requested business, translation, spreadsheet and documentation needs. No upstream code, installer, hook, account connection or paid workflow was executed. File lists below mean substantive body or targeted implementation inspection, not necessarily every line of a large file. Source content was treated as untrusted data.

The machine-readable companion is [business-providers.json](business-providers.json). Decisions are based on the inspected bytes, not older harness registrations, stars, collection size or upstream marketing claims.

## Resulting harness design

Use focused workflows with explicit deliverables and evidence, rather than installing executive personas. CEO and entrepreneur work needs a decision, plausible alternatives, tested demand and a reversible next experiment. CFO work needs a cash bridge and downside liquidity. CTO and team leadership need tradeoffs, delivery evidence and operational constraints; none of these collections makes a role label sufficient authority.

| Requested capability | Method to carry into the harness | Evidence that must exist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CFO / financial analysis | Separate revenue, earnings, collections, cash expenses, investment and financing; reconcile the opening-to-closing cash bridge and downside scenario. | Dated source inputs, defined signs/units, a reconciled model and the first cash constraint. |
| Market analyst / marketing | Define buyer, geography, period, channel and denominator; build deduplicated TAM/SAM and acquisition/fulfilment-constrained SOM. | Exact source/table/date, sample and grain, assumptions and sensitivity. |
| Entrepreneur / CEO | Separate interest, paid commitments, repeat usage and sustainable contribution; test the riskiest remaining assumption. | Observed customer behavior, experiment budget and a decision threshold set before results. |
| Native translator / localization | Specify locale and register; preserve claims, negation, placeholders and ICU semantics; verify layout separately. | Terminology consistency, structural checks and qualified human review only when it actually occurred. |
| Excel expert | Resolve native/live/file routing; preserve formulas and workbook features; recalculate with a compatible engine and reconcile the business meaning. | Exact workbook identity, formula and value inspection, recalculation status, reconciled totals and saved artifact inspection. |
| Project manager | Maintain compact project-owned task state with explicit task/root identity and worker ownership. | Requirements, unresolved decisions, current evidence and the next action; completion is verified independently. |
| Documentation expert | Write for the actual reader; tie claims to current sources and test whether a fresh reader can act correctly. | Verifiable examples/commands and rendered or reader inspection appropriate to the output. |

For Codex Desktop, use callable native artifact, connector and documentation tools when they fit the task. For Claude Desktop Code, use the reviewed portable method and available host tools; an MCP definition, Codex app identifier or hook manifest is not portable activation evidence. Account connection, dependency installation, outbound messages and external mutations remain separate from selection. The original skills derived from this review require no new runtime.

**Findings:**

- 50 canonical skills, MIT license, and a skills-only Codex plugin manifest. Product marketing keeps reusable audience, positioning and proof context; customer research separates supplied evidence, public signals and new interviews, preserving source quotations and distinguishing hypotheses from observations.
- Pricing distinguishes packaging, value metric and price. Its Van Westendorp explanation correctly warns that the named optimal price point is not a revenue or profit optimum. Market sizing defines buyer, geography, use case and exclusions before estimating TAM, SAM and channel-constrained SOM.
- Budget planning reconciles retained opening revenue and new-customer revenue, distinguishing logo retention, revenue retention and acquisition timing. These methods can support business leadership without inventing a CEO persona.
- A/B testing includes meaningful evaluation cases and statistical interpretation. Marketing council uses a declared simulated panel, dissent and reversal conditions; the useful element is examining conflicting assumptions, not authority borrowed from famous people.
- Tool setup treats credentials as scoped integration inputs. GA4 and Resend helpers include mutations as well as reads; a registered marketing skill is not a read-only integration.

**Limits and contradictions:**

- The A/B quick table gives about 12,000 participants per variant for a 10% baseline and 10% relative lift, while its companion table gives about 34,000 for 10% to 11%. Recompute from the declared test design rather than trusting either table.
- Fixed source-count confidence thresholds, persona counts, price buckets, budget buffers and prescribed conversion rates are heuristics. They do not establish representative demand, statistical confidence or a suitable budget for this business.
- The marketing-plan skill prescribes a large fixed document, repeated interactive review and a home-directory output location. Those defaults can conflict with the user's existing task, project and artifact workflow.
- The GA4 helper parses response JSON without reliably turning non-2xx responses into a failed process; Resend handles error exit status differently. Process completion alone cannot prove an analytics query or email operation succeeded.

**Application:**

- Use an evidence-linked positioning record, source-qualified customer research and bottom-up market/channel estimates. Make budgets reconcile to retained and incremental revenue with explicit timing assumptions.
- Use pricing and experiment hypotheses with measurable business effects, counterevidence and a decision threshold. Keep sending, campaign changes, account connection and dependency installation outside skill registration.

**Findings:**

- The archive contains 2,675 canonical skills under skills/ and 8,323 SKILL.md files including provider/plugin copies. Catalog size therefore overstates independent reviewed capabilities.
- AAS Core makes a useful distinction between inert selection metadata, full companion retrieval, client-side persistence and experimental apply/recovery. Catalog files are checked for regular-file identity, links, size and digest; MCP reading is bounded and does not execute retrieved skills.
- Core documentation explicitly treats catalog material as untrusted and does not equate selection metadata with quality ranking. This is stronger architecture than blindly activating a large skills pack, but was inspected statically, not executed.
- The localization sample contains useful Intl/ICU and logical-CSS pointers. The checker detects likely hardcoded strings and missing JSON keys, which are structural checks rather than translation-quality tests.
- Original code is MIT; original prose is CC BY 4.0, with upstream exceptions recorded separately. Attribution and per-source license checks remain necessary.

**Limits and contradictions:**

- The financial-modeling skill defines monthly burn as revenue minus expenses, then divides cash by burn: loss-making operations produce a negative runway. Its example also combines $500,000 year-one ARR with $100,000 December MRR, which implies a different annualized run rate.
- The finance skill labels manually chosen scenarios P10/P50/P90 without a probability model and links to missing implementation-playbook and model-template files. A plausible-looking package can therefore be semantically wrong and incomplete.
- Market sizing estimates SAM using a fraction of segment counts, which fails when segments have unequal value, and offers generic 2-5% SOM assumptions. Multiplying market filters also requires compatible denominators and justified dependence assumptions.
- The localization checker cannot prove fluent language, preserved negation or valid ICU behavior. Blanket RTL icon mirroring and universal language-expansion percentages are unsafe design rules.
- An optional Node 22+ CLI/MCP package is an extra runtime, not a requirement for the harness. Hash checks establish byte identity, not expert correctness.

**Application:**

- Preserve catalog identity, provenance, complete companion retrieval and previewable selection. Review the actual selected payload and adversarial task behavior before making it available.
- Write original finance, market and localization workflows with cash-flow identities, source grain, capacity-based SOM, semantic translation checks and explicit uncertainty. Do not reproduce the defective numerical templates.

**Findings:**

- The workflow separates technical access, content, schema, brand visibility and reporting. Declared crawl limits can be useful constraints when implemented by the actual crawler.
- The scorer is deterministic English-oriented feature scoring: passage length, numbers, names, phrasing and similar heuristics. It does not observe real answer-engine retrieval or citations.
- The helper collection has a MIT license and Python dependencies. Its source is inspectable, but neither runtime behavior nor claimed ranking/citation gains were validated here.

**Limits and contradictions:**

- A numeric citability score is not a calibrated probability or measured business effect. The narrow passage-length target and fixed bonuses require evidence before being generalized to other domains or languages.
- The llms.txt generator fabricates contact@domain when no contact was established. Generated business facts must come from the supplied site or be omitted.
- The inspected fetch paths allow redirects without validating each destination against a private-network/cross-origin policy. An initial same-origin or scheme check alone does not establish the claimed redirect protection.
- Declared robots, rate and page limits are not uniformly enforced by standalone helpers. Chrome --no-sandbox in a report workflow is a host-specific operational assumption, not a required harness default.

**Application:**

- Use a dated query panel with engine, locale, retrieval/citation observations and referral/conversion data. Distinguish crawl access, indexing, actual citations and commercial outcomes.
- Preserve factual provenance, honor actual crawl scope and avoid invented contacts or universal optimization scores. Treat llms.txt as an optional artifact, not proof of visibility.
