## Interpretation and retained limits

The two code tasks pass independent execution in both conditions. Research, memory,
UI/vector and lead-decision rubric scores tie. Most treatment cells use more tokens;
there is no demonstrated general quality or performance advantage. Two small ties
do not establish that an individual skill is universally useless. Retention rests
on concrete workflow/guardrails and explicit conditional routes, not catalog size.

mixed source-only exported video with live UI guidance. Baselines saved sources
that the native exporter refused; treatment exhausted its budget before saving.
Those failures remain in the initial results. The narrower expansion produced
all four source artifacts. Three exported; the fourth exposed an independently
reproduced Darwin owned-child exit race. No source was repaired to conceal it.

condition mapping or author histories. Code checks were executable; structured
answer checks and visual review used frozen rubrics. The lead viewed saved neutral
UI/vector pixels before reading the condition mapping. Initial and changed evidence
are retained, including reviewer uncertainty. Review scores are descriptive, not
statistical proof or universal capability measurements.

activation was independently tested, but the initial screenshots precede focus/
