## Privacy

Installing the harness does not automatically send your local project files or
local memory to Mirket Software. Models and tools you configure may send selected
information to their providers. Review those providers' policies before sharing
confidential or personal information.

The harness website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies in
its own code. It runs on Hetzner Cloud in Nuremberg, Germany. The server keeps
operational access and error logs for delivery, troubleshooting and security.
These may contain IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps and browser details.
Logs rotate daily with 14 retained rotations; this is not a guarantee that all
copies are deleted within 14 days.

Messages sent to our contact address are handled to respond to your request.
Incoming email is routed through Apple iCloud Mail. You may contact us about
access, correction or deletion of information we hold about you; applicable
rights and exceptions depend on the relevant law.
