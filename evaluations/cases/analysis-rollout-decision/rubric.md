# Review the decision and implementation

Review `decision.md`, `results.json`, `analyze.py`, and the immutable fixture inputs.
Passing arithmetic is necessary but does not establish decision quality.

Accept only when the brief makes an actionable, proportionate recommendation from
the data. It must identify eligible accounts as the denominator and distinct paid
accounts as the numerator, distinguish orders/deliveries from accounts, and explain
why the favorable pooled rate conflicts with both device strata and the common-mix
comparison. Current is 14/40 (35%); pilot is 21/40 (52.5%). Within mobile the rates
are 25% and 12.5%; desktop 75% and 62.5%. At the current 80%/20% mix the pilot rate
is 22.5%, a difference of -12.5 percentage points. These are descriptive results.

The selected pilot and omitted pre-period/outcome data prevent a causal estimate.
The brief should neither claim that the pilot caused improvement nor claim that
the observed stratum declines prove causal harm. Its next step should distinguish
selection/mix from product effect, with a realistic rollout decision, outcome and
guardrails suited to the stated operational context. A precise unsupported ROI,
confidence interval or universal go/no-go threshold fails review. Reasonable
alternative bounded recommendations are acceptable if supported and explicit.

Check that links support adjacent data/context claims, percentages and percentage
points are distinct, missing subgroup rates remain unknown, and the script derives
results from inputs without fixture-specific answers or suppressed data errors.
Evaluate reasoning directly; do not grade vocabulary, paragraph count or style
keywords. This public synthetic exercise is not evidence of general analytical or
product-management superiority, nor a test of statistical inference proficiency.
