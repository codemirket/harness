## Next bounded deliverable

Build one original landing page for a fictional activity product, with its own
identity, copy and assets. Use Granger as the user's aesthetic comparison;
retain Hallmark/Taste as method resources. Do not force the prior Foldway icon
brief to stand in for an entire page. Start with the hero and two distinct
supporting sections before expanding scope.

The page and its presentation are separate outputs:

1. A responsive, usable page whose static hero and representative content
   sections are compelling at desktop and mobile. Create or select suitable
   original/licensed imagery rather than approximating every textured subject
   with elementary SVG shapes. Use SVG for appropriate icons and editable
   graphics, not as an imposed medium for all imagery.
2. An editable HyperFrames reel presenting the same page/artwork at a declared
   production frame rate, initially 30fps. Specify focal subject, layer order,
   transition purpose and reading holds before animating. Cinematic viewport
   movement belongs to this reel unless it also serves the actual interface.

Use project CSS/WAAPI or already approved libraries for live UI motion; Mirket
handles its qualified design and HyperFrames export routes. Existing raster
creation tools may supply new imagery when suitable. No new dependency or

## Evidence and acceptance

Freeze a concrete brief, assets/rights, viewport/export profile, initial source,
quality rubric and one shared production/review/repair budget before authoring.
Keep a capable lead and comparable independent reviewer. The initial review
must address composition and distinctive visual direction, not just clipping
or successful export. Review against the user-selected reference at comparable
viewing scale without claiming a pixel match or copying its identity.

Inspect static hero/section pixels before adding movement. Then inspect named
motion beats and saved decoded exports, verify required properties survived
browser parsing, and check actual normal-speed playback separately. Test the
live page's keyboard/focus, responsive layout, reduced motion and interruption.
A promotional reel does not prove the page works; a functional page does not
prove the reel has good pacing. Keep initial findings, one targeted repair
where justified, final artifacts and remaining defects. Do not carry this single
reference into universal rules for all project skills or mark catalog acceptance
complete from the plan.
