# Diagram brief: request relay

A fictional local service receives a request, verifies its details, then
resolves it. Create a standalone, editable animated diagram that communicates
the sequence **Intake → Verify → Resolve**. The graphic will sit in a public
website hero at a 760px desktop width and must remain useful in a 320px viewport.

Use original HTML/CSS/SVG/JavaScript and no new dependency. Show the factual
route and labels without scripting or motion. Motion should clarify the order
and finish in a meaningful resting state; provide a replay control and respect
reduced motion on load and during a live preference change. Preserve semantic
text and sensible keyboard behavior. Deliver the source and a concise note on
the intended visual and motion decisions. Render and inspect the actual result
at both widths and normal speed before handing it off.

This is a fictional evaluation input, not a Mirket product claim or approved
brand direction. Do not reuse the specimen's names or distinctive appearance.
