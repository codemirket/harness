opened. These findings establish neither universal accessibility nor design quality.

events and six complete route stdout/stderr pairs. Required guidance, source read

selected one lesson; delivery does not prove attention or transfer.

artifact and later independent browser evidence remain available, but the original
failed reports are absent. Its evidence-integrity/execution flags therefore fail.
This is an evidence-retention failure, not an inferred product defect or cap overshoot.

absolute path bindings, while the frozen strict serializer expects exact keys and
project-relative bindings. The unchanged packet verifier rejects the lock format;
the unchanged primary analyzer returns `normal relative path required`. Originals
were kept intact. A separate independent exact-rational calculation retained all
8 ratings and 40 dimension values; its descriptive estimate does not repair or
replace the unavailable primary. The numerical thresholds fail independently of
these format and retention issues.

## What this supports

The five-file teaching/routing revision addressed local comparison, feedback and
reference-chaining failures. The library retains 27 source/license records and its
worked examples. This run supports their availability and evaluated behavior;
it does not establish a measured quality benefit or a universal design language.
Earlier negative/incomplete broad, transfer and scoped studies remain separate;
none was pooled into this estimate or relabeled a confirmation.

The current user direction is to keep references as optional resources for
inspiration, validation, comparison and review, with model-led exploration beyond
the builtin library. The subsequent autonomy/duplication cleanup is a separate
source revision and inherits no quality-gain claim from this comparison.
