# Repair Cafe

Create a warm editorial illustration of two adults repairing a small desk lamp at a community repair cafe. One holds the lamp base steady; the other adjusts a screw with a screwdriver. Show believable hand/tool contact and a clear shared action. Use a tactile painterly treatment, restrained ochre, teal and warm off-white, no lettering. Deliver a 1200x800 PNG and a 600x400 placement proof. Avoid gear, lightbulb-idea and floating-interface symbolism.
