# Craft review protocol

Use two reviewers for a consequential comparative evaluation, independently and
with randomized neutral output labels. Each sees the raw brief, final full-size,
placement-size and focal-detail pixels, and the same relevant reference set.
Do not disclose condition, creator explanations or the other reviewer's ratings
before the first ratings are fixed. This separation does not prove human expertise.

Calibrate first using a deliberately flawed and a corrected composition in two
different visual languages. Also show an identical output under different labels.
Reviewers must locate the intended flaws and explain why they matter. If they
reward the flaw or prefer identical pixels for invented reasons, resolve the
measurement problem before making a gain claim; preserve the failed calibration.

Rate each lens 0–4: 0 unusable, 1 major defects, 2 serviceable with visible
weaknesses, 3 convincing with minor weaknesses, 4 exceptional fit and execution.
Scores require a visible location and reason. Do not award points for fashionable
style, resemblance to one reference, tool choice or verbose process notes.

| Lens | What the rating measures |
| --- | --- |
| Concept | Specific, legible visual idea that serves this brief |
| Composition | Focal hierarchy, silhouette, rhythm, negative space and crop |
| Construction | Anatomy/contact or geometry, perspective, light, edges and material |
| Coherence | Deliberate relationship among style, image, typography and family members |
| Placement fit | Audience/brand suitability and clarity in each required real use |

Record strengths, up to three high-impact defects, unresolved uncertainty and an
overall preference (`A`, `B`, `tie`, or `unjudgeable`) with rationale. Permit ties.
Lock these observations before reading source or guardrail results. Then check
editability, exact content, rights/provenance and functional correctness separately.
A craft average cannot cancel a consequential failure. Record actual reviewer
identity/type, output hashes, displayed order and whether condition was guessed.

Before actual trials, freeze the practical decision threshold and handling of
missing outcomes in the experiment record. Use the existing design-quality
protocol's approach to calibration, counterbalancing and disagreement rather than
inventing a new quality certification. Report original scores when reviewers
disagree; a coordinating review may explain the dispute but must not overwrite it.
These fixtures have no observed scores and no claim of gain.
